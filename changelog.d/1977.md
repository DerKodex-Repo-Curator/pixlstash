- The Run popup's *Add ‹trigger word›* button puts the word where the cursor
  is in the prompt, so it can go mid-sentence; with the cursor at the start (or
  never placed) it still goes first.
- Typing a person's trigger word into the Run popup's prompt picks that person
  and adds their LoRA, and typing their name puts their trigger word in its
  place. Each person's face in the Person field tells their trigger word on
  hover.
