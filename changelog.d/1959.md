  `qwen_image_edit_…` is Qwen-Image-Edit rather than Qwen-Image, `pony_v7_…` is

  `ltx_2_…` or `sd_3_5_…` now read as HunyuanImage, LTX-2 and SD 3.5. Files
  already on the shelf are corrected when PixlStash starts.
