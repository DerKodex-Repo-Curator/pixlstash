- Open in ComfyUI now puts in the PixlStash loaders for more of the graph.
  Load VAE and Load CLIP load their file off your model shelf, and so does
  Load Diffusion Model when ComfyUI runs on the same computer. Load Image
  becomes the PixlStash Picture Loader: on its picture where that is in your
  library, otherwise empty for you to browse in. Save Video becomes the
  PixlStash Video Saver on newer ComfyUI versions too, encoding with the
  saver's default codec in place of the one the node had.
