- A stack's order can now be found and changed without dragging. Right-click a
  picture of a stack and choose **Edit stack** to make it the cover, move it
  earlier or later, take it out of the stack, unstack the whole stack, or keep
  the cover only. In an open stack every picture shows its place, the cover a
  star, and hovering one shows move and Make cover buttons; Ctrl+←, Ctrl+→ and
  Ctrl+Home do the same from the keyboard. The lightbox shows "2 of 4 in this
  stack" with a Make cover button, and the filmstrip gets the same buttons.
