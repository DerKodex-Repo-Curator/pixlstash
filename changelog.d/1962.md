- The Kind on a Models row is now a menu: pick a kind right on the row instead
  of going through Set kind…. When every workflow that loads an Unclassified
  model (or one filed as a checkpoint) loads it as a text encoder or a VAE, the
  row suggests that kind under the chip, and one choice files it. Nothing is
  re-filed until you choose, and "Don't suggest a kind" stops the suggestion.
- The Workflow tab offers the same fix under a text encoder or VAE picker, and
  its text encoder row is now called Text encoder instead of CLIP.
- When a workflow's text encoder or VAE is missing and your shelf has the same
  file under another name but filed as the wrong kind, the Workflow tab offers
  to file it right, and picks it for that row in the same choice when the
  loader can load it.
