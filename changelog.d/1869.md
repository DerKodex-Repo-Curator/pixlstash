- Hiding a workflow is each library's own choice, not the whole machine's:
  hide the workflows that do not belong in a library and they stay off the
  Workflows grid there, while every other library keeps them. Workflow sets
  hide the same way - the choice belongs to the library you are in, so a set
  (hand-made or found from your pictures) left out of one library is still
  there in the others.
