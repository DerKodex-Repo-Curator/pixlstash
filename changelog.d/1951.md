- Workflow cards draw a model PixlStash recognises by name: a base model's
  tile carries its maker's icon (Flux, Qwen, Stability, Krea and more) in the
  maker's colour, with its version on a pill at the top corner. Fine-tunes
  and LoRAs wear their base model's tile; the tab at the bottom corner tells
  them apart (a fine-tune's own initials, LORA), says which release a base model
  is (BASE, REFI, EDIT, RAW), or shows a lightning bolt for a few-step model,
  with the step count on a speed LoRA.
- FLUX.2 Klein, Ideogram 4 and SeedVR2 are recognised as base models.
