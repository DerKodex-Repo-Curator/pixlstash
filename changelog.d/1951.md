- Workflow cards draw a model PixlStash recognises by name: a base model's
  tile carries its maker's icon (Flux, Qwen, Stability, Krea and more), its
  name (Dev, Klein, SDXL) and its version, in the maker's colour. Fine-tunes
  and LoRAs wear their base model's tile; the tab tells them apart (a
  fine-tune's own initials, LORA), says which release a base model is (BASE,
  REFI, EDIT, RAW), or shows a lightning bolt for a few-step model, with the
  step count on a speed LoRA.
- FLUX.2 Klein, Ideogram 4 and SeedVR2 are recognised as base models.
