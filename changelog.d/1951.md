- Workflow cards draw a model PixlStash recognises by name: a base model's
  tile carries its maker's icon (Flux, Qwen, Stability, Krea and more), its
  name (Dev, Klein, SDXL) and its version, in the maker's colour; a LoRA shows
  its base model's name over its own initials. A tab says which release it is
  (BASE, REFI, EDIT, RAW), and a lightning bolt marks a few-step model, with
  the step count on a speed LoRA.
- FLUX.2 Klein, Ideogram 4 and SeedVR2 are recognised as base models.
