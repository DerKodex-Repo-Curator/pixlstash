- A workflow is now edited where you read it. In the Workflow tab every model
  row is a picker, stages are checkboxes, a LoRA's strength is set on its row
  and a × removes its loader. Changes wait as one unsaved set, shown as a bar
  above *Run…* with *Discard*, **Save as new…** and, where the changes can be
  saved over the workflow, **Save**. The card wears an *Unsaved* badge until
  then. *Run with changes…* tries them first.
- **Save** writes over the workflow and keeps the graph it replaces. Once a
  workflow has more than one version, the inspector head shows the one you
  are on and opens a menu of the earlier ones; choosing one goes back to it,
  and nothing is removed, so going back can be undone.
- The Run popup offers to keep what you changed for a run: a band above its
  footer counts what differs from the workflow, with **Save** and **Save as
  new…**. After a run that changed something, the Tasks tab makes the same
  offer, so you can decide once the pictures are back. Saving from there saves
  what the run used: anything you changed in the Workflow tab after the run
  stays waiting there, and the save says so, or names what it could not keep.
  Prompt, seed, count and the people picked always stay with the run.
- The Run popup's *Person* field picks several people. Each adds their LoRA as
  a row of its own, and the trigger-word reminder is one line with a button
  for each word the prompt lacks, or one *Add all* button when more than three
  are missing.
- Changing a workflow's checkpoint to another one you have installed can only
  be saved as a new workflow; replacing one that is missing is saved over it.
- *Clone onto a set…* is now *Use a set…*, which fills the model rows as
  unsaved changes. *Save fixed workflow* and the "Replaced" notice are gone,
  and *Edit LoRAs…* no longer asks whether to write a new workflow.
