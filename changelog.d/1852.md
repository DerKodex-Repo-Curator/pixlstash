- A workflow opened in ComfyUI from PixlStash now saves back to itself. Press
  Ctrl+S in ComfyUI and the workflow gets a new version in PixlStash, with no
  filename to pick and no question to answer; ComfyUI says which version was
  saved, and the Workflow tab says "Saved from ComfyUI" with *Go back* beside
  it. The button is now *Open in ComfyUI*, without "a copy".
- A workflow you arranged in ComfyUI opens the way you left it: node
  positions, groups and notes are kept with the workflow and come back the
  next time you open it from PixlStash.
- A workflow opened in ComfyUI gets a PixlStash loader beside its model for
  trying it on people. Pick one or several; what you pick stays in ComfyUI and
  is never saved into the workflow.
