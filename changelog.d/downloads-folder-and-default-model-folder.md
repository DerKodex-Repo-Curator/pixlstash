- The Model folders dialog can watch your Downloads folder. A `.safetensors`
  or `.gguf` model you download there is moved into your default model folder
  once it has finished downloading, and appears on the model shelf by itself,
  with a `new` tag on Models in the sidebar until you look.
- You can choose the default model folder in the Model folders dialog. Models
  you add with Add file, and models from your Downloads folder, go there.
